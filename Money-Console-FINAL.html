What happens if the $500 never lands

The honest trade on the necklace

Because the pawn runs 40–45 days, redemption lands around October 7 — not September. So September ends the same either way. The cost shows up in October, your three-paycheck month, and it pushes the date you are finally a full month ahead from December into January. If the choice is pawn or eviction, pawn. One month of delay is a fair price for keeping the roof. But ask the shop about an interest-only extension before you sign — most Florida shops allow it, and it is your safety valve if October runs tight.

Rent Savings Account — the real picture

Rent is paid OUT of this account every month. Whatever a month has left over goes back IN. The balance is not spare money — it is next month's rent arriving early.

The finish line, stated plainly

You are truly ahead the first month you can pay rent entirely from this account before a single paycheck arrives — and still have money left. Until then, the balance is float, not savings.
