Before you spend a dollar

In this order. The first two matter more than the money.

1Call legal aid Monday morning Ask two things: how many days do I actually have, and is there a defect in the notice? A Florida 3-day notice can only list unpaid rent — no late fees, no utilities.
2Check the court docket for your address Confirm what has actually been filed.
3File your written response by ~Sept 5 This is the one deadline that effort cannot fix.
4Confirm the $500 lands Monday Everything below it in the plan depends on that deposit.
5Ask the pawn shop about an interest-only extension Before you sign, not after. It's your safety valve if October runs tight.
